Symbolic Music Generation

Melody–Chord Co-generation

Music X Lab
MBZUAI × NYU

Each system is given a 5-bar prompt and jointly generates the continuation of both the melody and its underlying chord progression. We draw 3 samples per song for every system (use ❮ ❯ to switch). Evaluated on 92 held-out POP909 songs, each shown as the prompt plus ~22 generated bars, played through piano. Click or drag a roll to seek.
