Symbolic Music Generation

Melody–Chord Co-generation

Each system is given a 5-bar prompt and jointly generates the continuation of both the melody and its underlying chord progression. We draw 3 samples per song for every system; use ❮ ❯ to switch between them. Evaluated on 92 held-out POP909 songs (identified by their dataset index), each shown as the prompt plus ~22 generated bars, played through piano at 120 BPM.
