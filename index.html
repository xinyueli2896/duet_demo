DUET: Coordinating Pretrained Language Models for Joint Generation of Melody and Harmony

Xinyue Li1   Gus Xia1   Ziyu Wang1,2
1 Computing and Mathematical Sciences Division, MBZUAI, Abu Dhabi, UAE
2 Courant Institute, New York University, New York, United States
xinyue.li@mbzuai.ac.ae  ·  gus.xia@mbzuai.ac.ae  ·  ziyu.wang@nyu.edu

We introduce DUET, a framework that integrates two pretrained autoregressive models into a single Transformer whose architecture supports cross-stream communication at every layer, enabling coordinated generation of time-aligned output pairs.

We compare DUET against two internal baselines — single-stream Transformers trained from scratch and fine-tuned from a pretrained checkpoint — and two external baselines, Whole-Song Generation and the Anticipatory Music Transformer (references below). Each system generates continuations for 92 MIDIs from the POP909 test set, each with aligned melody and chord tracks: given a 5-bar prompt, every model produces a 22-bar continuation (3 samples per song; use ❮ ❯ to switch). The prompt is the shaded region on each piano roll — click anywhere on a roll or drag the playhead to start playing.
